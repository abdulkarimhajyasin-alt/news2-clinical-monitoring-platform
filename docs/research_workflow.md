# Research Workflow

```text
Patient Baseline
↓
Dialysis Session
↓
Repeated Monitoring
↓
NEWS2 Calculation
↓
Alert if NEWS2 >= 5 or sudden deterioration
↓
Deterioration Log
↓
Medical/Nursing Response
↓
Response Time Tracking
↓
Outcome within 24-72h
↓
Research Dataset
```

Phase 02 creates the database structures for this workflow. Later phases should implement validated NEWS2 calculation, workflow rules, export controls, and UI/API integration.
